About the Company 

Sales'Up is a leading Creatio implementation partner, helping enterprise clients across FMCG, Agro, Finance and Pharma transform their business processes. Over 8+ years, we've delivered 100+ successful CRM projects and built our own industry-specific products. 

Join us to work on complex, real-world enterprise challenges and grow together! 

 

Role 

We are looking for a Junior Business Systems Analyst to join our team and work at the intersection of business needs and system configuration. You'll help gather and clarify requirements from enterprise clients, work hands-on with the system, and dive into data and dashboards — growing step by step into a full business analyst role on projects for market leaders in FMCG, Agro and Finance. 

 

Main Responsibilities 

  • Get familiar with existing system logic and handle simple to medium configuration tasks based on defined specs 
  • Verify that configured scenarios actually work from the end-user's perspective 
  • Help gather and clarify requirements from clients — ask the right follow-up questions when a task is underspecified 
  • Understand the business problem behind each request and connect it to process, users, or expected outcome 
  • Work with analytical data: navigate large data volumes, build and read dashboards 
  • Provide realistic time estimates for tasks and own those estimates 
  • Document what was done and flag open questions 
  • Communicate calmly and clearly with clients and the internal team 

 

Requirements 

  • Analytical thinking — experience working with large data volumes, ability to navigate and draw conclusions from data 
  • Experience working with dashboards and analytics tools 
  • Ability to give realistic estimates on tasks 
  • Attention to detail and independence 
  • Willingness to ask questions when something's unclear, rather than guessing 
  • Basic technical background, ideally with some exposure to CRM configuration 
  • Comfortable working directly with business users 

 

Nice to Have 

  • Experience configuring CRM/BPM/ERP or low-code/no-code systems (Creatio/Terrasoft is a plus) 
  • Background as a junior BA, functional consultant, CRM consultant, or implementation specialist 
  • Understanding of sales, service, or document workflow processes 

 

We Offer 

  • Professional development through internal and external training programs 
  • Transparent model of professional and career growth 
  • Option to work in the office or remotely 
  • 24 days of paid vacation 
  • English language courses
