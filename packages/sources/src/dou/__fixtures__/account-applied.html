We sincerely appreciate your interest in Wetelo and hope to stay in touch!

Please include your salary expectations (monthly, USD) in your application.

Currently, we are looking for a Middle JavaScript Full Stack Developer (Node + React) to join our team. Back-end development will be the primary focus of this role, while the developer will also contribute to the front-end when needed. This role is a great fit for someone who enjoys building systems from scratch and collaborating in a product-oriented team.

What you will do:
— designing and developing REST APIs
— building user-facing features using React.js
— developing admin panels and internal tools
— backend architecture setup from scratch
— integrations with payment systems and third-party services
— integrations with AI tools and external APIs
— database design and optimization
— working closely with developers, QA and client
— participating in technical decisions and improving system architecture
— writing clean, testable, and well-documented code

Technical stack:
— Node.js
— Nest.js
— React.js
— Next.js
— TypeScript
— HTML, CSS
— PostgreSQL / MongoDB

What we expect from you:
— at least 4 years of experience in web development, with a minimum of 3 years as a Full Stack Developer in an outsourcing or outstaffing company
— strong expertise in Node.js with hands-on experience in React.js and TypeScript
— experience working with Nest.js and Next.js
— solid understanding of HTML, CSS and JavaScript
— Experience working with PostgreSQL or MongoDB
— familiarity with REST APIs and GraphQL
— understanding of authentication and authorization (JWT, RBAC)
— experience integrating third-party services or payment systems
— knowledge of database design and query optimization
— experience using AI tools in daily development workflow (e.g., Claude Code, Codex or similar)
— clean, maintainable, and scalable code practices
— ability to take ownership and deliver end-to-end solutions
— strong problem-solving skills and attention to detail
— English level: B2

Nice to have:
— Basic DevOps knowledge (Docker, CI/CD)
— Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science or related field

What we offer
— supportive and comfortable working environment
— paid annual vacation (18 working days per year)
— paid sick leave (including up to 7 days without a medical certificate)
— all official public holidays are days off (+11 days per year)
— free online speaking club to practice your English
— training and self-development opportunities
— team-building events and corporate parties
— accounting support and additional perks

The stages of the hiring process: Pre-screen interview with a Recruiter → Technical interview → Final interview with the company’s CEO → References → Job Offer
